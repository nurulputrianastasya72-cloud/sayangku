About You 💗

There are so many things I could say about you, but sometimes I still feel that words are not enough to explain how meaningful you have become in my life. We have never met each other in person, and yet, somehow, you have become someone very close to my heart. Our story may be different from other people's stories. We cannot simply meet whenever we miss each other, spend an afternoon together, or create memories side by side in the same place. But despite all of that, you have always found your own ways to make me feel remembered, cared for, and appreciated. You are a kind person, and I notice the little things you do. I notice your effort. I notice the time you give me, even when you are tired. I notice how you still try to make time for me in the middle of your daily responsibilities.

Maybe you do not always realize how much those little things mean to me. But I do. Thank you for being someone who has brought so many meaningful moments into my life. Thank you for your patience, your attention, your kindness, and for every effort you have made, even when things were not always easy. I hope you know that you are appreciated, not because you are perfect, but because you are you.

💗

Our Little Everyday Moments ☀️

Some of my favorite memories with you are actually the simplest ones. The way you wake up and call me before starting your day. The way you go to work and then come back and call me again. Those little moments may look ordinary to someone else, but to me, they became a beautiful part of my everyday life. Sometimes we do not even need to talk about something important. Sometimes just hearing your voice, knowing how your day went, or simply spending a little time together is already enough to make an ordinary day feel different. I know you have your own responsibilities, your own tiredness, your own problems, and your own things to think about. That is why I appreciate every moment you choose to share with me. It is not about how long our conversations are. It is about the fact that, in the middle of your busy day, you still make room for me.

Those little calls, conversations, jokes, silences, and random moments have become memories that I treasure deeply. Maybe someday, when we look back at this chapter of our lives, we will realize that these simple moments were actually some of the most precious ones.

The Little Things That Mean So Much 💗

Sometimes, it is not the big things that mean the most. It is the little things. The way you call me when you wake up. The way you still make time for me before going to work. The way you call me again when you get home, even after a long and tiring day. We have never met in person, but somehow, you have still managed to make me feel close to you. Every call, every conversation, every random story, every “How was your day?”, and every moment you choose to spend with me has become something I treasure.

Maybe these things seem ordinary to you. Maybe they are just small parts of your everyday routine. But to me, they mean so much. I notice your efforts. I notice how you try to make time for me even when you are busy or tired. And I want you to know that I never take those little things for granted.

Thank you for letting me be a part of your everyday life. Thank you for every morning call, every after-work conversation, every laugh, every silly moment, and even the quiet moments when we simply stay on the call together.

We may not have met face to face yet, but the memories we have created are real and meaningful to me. Sometimes, love is not found in grand gestures. Sometimes, it is found in the little things someone does without even realizing how much they mean. And for me, those little things are some of the most beautiful parts of us. 💗

The Difficult Days 🌧️

I also want to be honest about something. Lately, things have not been easy for us. We have been arguing more often than before. Sometimes we stop talking for hours. Sometimes emotions become too strong, and instead of understanding each other, we end up misunderstanding each other even more. There are moments when I wish we could simply pause, take a breath, and remember that we are on the same side. I know that I am not always right. I know that I have things I need to improve too. Sometimes I may speak from my emotions instead of taking the time to understand yours. And I am sorry for the moments when my words or actions have made things harder.

I do not want our problems to become something that makes us forget all the good things we have shared. I hope that, as we grow, we can become more mature when facing difficult situations. I hope we can learn to communicate instead of staying silent for too long, listen instead of immediately defending ourselves, and solve problems instead of allowing them to become bigger.

We do not have to be perfect. I only hope that when things become difficult, we can choose patience, honesty, respect, and understanding. Because sometimes loving someone also means learning how to handle disagreements without losing kindness toward each other.

🌷

Distance & Our Differences 🌏

There is another part of our story that makes everything more complicated. We come from different backgrounds. I am Muslim, while you are Buddhist. You are Chinese, and your family has hopes and expectations about the person you will eventually be with. I understand that family matters. I understand that parents have their own beliefs, hopes, traditions, and dreams for their children. I also understand that our differences are not something that can simply disappear because we care about each other. Sometimes, caring about someone means acknowledging reality instead of pretending that every obstacle is easy. Our distance is real. Our differences are real. The challenges from our families are real.

But so are the memories we have created, the effort we have given each other, and the feelings that have grown between us. I do not know exactly what the future has prepared for us. I cannot promise that everything will always be easy. But I hope that whatever happens, we will continue to treat each other with kindness and respect. For me, what matters is that the time we have shared has been meaningful. And even though we have never stood beside each other in the same place, you have still managed to leave a meaningful place in my heart.

Let’s Take It One Step at a Time

We don't have to have all the answers right now. We are both still learning, still growing, and still trying to understand each other. There will be good days and difficult days, moments when we feel close and moments when we need a little time to breathe. I know our journey is not always easy. There are many things we have to face, and sometimes it can feel overwhelming. But I don't want us to rush through everything.

Let's take it one step at a time. Let's learn to listen instead of assuming. Let's learn to talk instead of staying silent for too long. Let's learn to understand instead of immediately getting angry. Let's learn to apologize, forgive, and grow from the things we go through.

We don't need to be perfect. We just need to keep learning how to treat each other with kindness, patience, and understanding. Maybe we don't know exactly where this journey will take us, but for now, let's simply take the next step with care. 💗
